File prep

Make your file
press-ready.

Color: CMYK is the language of print

Set your document and images to CMYK. For brand colors, include Pantone references or a physical sample so we can match the result with confidence.

Bleed: give the edge some room

Extend artwork 3mm beyond the trim edge on all sides. Keep important text and logos at least 3mm inside the trim line.

Format: vector when it matters

PDF/X-1a is our preferred delivery format. Supply logos and line art as vector files, and embed or outline all fonts before export.

Images: quality you can see

Use images at 300dpi at final size. We can review your file before production and flag anything that might compromise the final result.
